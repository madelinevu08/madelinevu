Caring For Your Exotic Pet 

 
 
 

Caring for an exotic pet may be a new experience for you. It takes a true commitment from the owner. With thoughtful preparation, you can enjoy life with a rare animal and they can live long, healthy lives. There are many sources of excellent exotic pet care advice.

Tips and Tricks

  • Exotic pets may not always mix well with children or other pets. Supervise interactions carefully, especially with animals like large reptiles or birds.
  • Exotic pets, such as reptiles, can carry salmonella, so wash your hands thoroughly after handling them or cleaning their habitat.
  • Create an environment that mimics their natural conditions, including temperature, humidity, light, and substrate.
  • Maintain a clean habitat by removing waste and uneaten food to prevent bacterial growth, mold, or disease.
  • Exotic pets can hide illness, so monitor their behavior closely for changes in appetite, activity level, or appearance.
  • Some exotic pets enjoy being handled, but others may become stressed. Learn how to handle your pet properly and understand when to give them space.
