Web Design Vocabulary

Persona
A fictional character which represents certain traits and qualities of real users
Demographics
The measurable characteristics of a user group, such as age, gender, income, education level, location, and occupation
Psychographics
The study and classification of people based on their attitudes, aspirations, and other psychological criteria
Symmetry
A visual balance designers achieve by arranging elements to mirror each other or follow a consistent pattern
Proximity
Place related elements near each other and unrelated elements apart
Continuity
Guide users' eyes in a certain direction—a point that makes it easier for them to navigate the interface
Color Theory
A concept used in visual arts and design that explains how colors interact with each other and how they can be combined to create certain feelings, moods, and reactions
Grid System
It helps you align screen elements based on sequenced columns and rows
Hierarchy
The arrangement or organization of elements within a design in a way that guides the viewer's eye through the content in a specific order of importance
Accessibility
The practice of making information, activities, and/or environments sensible, meaningful, and usable for as many people as possible
Hick's Law
The more choices users face, the longer it will take them to make a decision
Fitt's Law
The time to move to a target depends on how big it is and on how far away it is
Discoverability
How easily users can understand, find, identify, and access a design product's features, functions, or content
Functionality
Whether a design works and helps the users meet their goals and needs
Aesthetics
A core design principle that defines a design's pleasing qualities like balance, color, movement, pattern, scale, shape and visual weight
