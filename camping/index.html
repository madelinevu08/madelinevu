A Camper's Dream Island Getaway

Channel Island National Park consists of 5 different islands 22 miles off the coast of Santa Barbara. The Channel Islands provide a great camping experience offering scuba diving, surfing, hiking, and a quiet, relaxing vacation. There is an incredible view of the ocean with over 2000 plants and animals you can see!

Camping

Pricing:

  • Free Entry
  • Free ferry transportation to each island
  • Reservations are $15 per night-per site.

Our 5 Campgrounds:

There is one campground on each island!

  • Above the Landing Cove on Santa Barbara
  • On the east islet of Anacapa
  • At Scorpion Ranch of Santa Cruz
  • At Water Canyon on Santa Rosa
  • Above Cuyler Harbor

Best Times to Camp:

We recommend staying 2-3 days for a weekend island getaway!

  • Winter is definitely the coldest season to go! The waters are rough, but it is the best time to see Northern Elephant seals and California brown pelicans!
  • Spring is the most beautiful season to visit. The wildflowers start to bloom and the island is greenest it will be all year!
  • Summer is most popular season to go with the warm waters for snorkeling, whale watching, and nice weather.
  • Fall is a great time to go with the cooler weather (which makes it a great season for hiking!) and calm waters!

Activities:

  • Scuba Diving, Boating, Fishing, Tidepooling, Snorkeling, Surfing, and Kayaking
  • Whale Watching, Seal and Sea Lion Watching, and Bird Watching (Certain animals only come during certain seasons)
  • Hiking and Picnicking

Tips

  1. Pack everything - there are no hotels, or stores in the area.
  2. You can only get to the islands from the sea or sky. There are ferrys that go to each island! (Check the schedule!)
  3. Visit during the summmer - blue and humpback whales are migrating past the islands!
  4. Take a kayak tour to learn more about the islands!
  5. The park’s waters are great for exploring wildlife. There are kelp forests and thousands of sea plants and animals to see!
  6. Bring more than enough food for your stay!

Fun Facts About The Channel Islands

  1. The Painted Cave on Santa Cruz Island inspired some of the Pirates of the Caribbean Movies.
  2. In 1959, Archaelogists found the oldest human remains on Santa Rosa Island.
  3. There are over 20 species unique to the Channel Islands.
  4. The tallest peak is called Devil's Peak. It is located on Santa Cruz Island and is over 2400 feet tall.
  5. Another name for the Channel Islands is "The Galapagos of North America."
