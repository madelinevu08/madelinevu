About Us

On our site, you will find the most popular travel destinations as well as unique and exotic luxury experiences.

See The World Travel Agency was opened in 2019 by Katharine Sargent after discovering her true
passion was travel. Sargent left the busy world of technology and decided to share her
knowledge and expertise with others that seek the world of travel adventures. See The World Travel
blends exciting travel opportunities with the efficiency of at home shopping on the web.
