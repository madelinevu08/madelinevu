A Taste of Italy: A Traveler's Dream Food Tour 

Foodie alert! The following information will make you crave Italian food immediately after reading. With the guidance of an experienced and well known chef, you will travel through cities in Italy, learning about the food of the region while immersing yourself in the preparation. You will enjoy farm tours only meant for the insiders, private tastings and of course hands-on cooking classes. Fresh cheeses, delicate pastas, and farm to table meats and produce will be enjoyed. Luxury accommodations at gorgeous villas and vineyard estates are all included in this delectable package.

HIGHLIGHTS: 

  1. Gelato Tasting: Travelers visit many gelaterias to see all that Italy has to offer.
  2. Wine in Tuscany: Calling all wine enthusiasts! We visit 2 of the most famous wineries in the world. See how they make their fantastic wine on this exclusive tour.
  3. Learn New Recipes: Learn over 10 new recipes on this trip as you learn to make the best pizza, pasta, and more!

The Wildlife of Africa: An Unforgettable Safari

There is nothing like the experience of seeing powerful, wild animals in their spectacular habitat of Africa. Depending on the time of year, you may witness the migration of the wildebeest, observe chimpanzees and gorillas in the jungles or watch the magnificent rhinos wade through streams. For the person looking for an unforgettable and exciting adventure, the African Safari tours are created for you! Your package will include transportation throughout the adventure and safe, comfortable accommodations with a veteran guide of the African wilderness.

HIGHLIGHTS:

  1. Diverse Wildlife: Come See the World's wildlife on this exclusive animal tour. You'll see elephants, giraffes, and many of the world's greatest animals.
  2. Expert Guides: See the World's expert safari guides will take you on a once-in-a-lifetime tour to see the animals up close and personal!
  3. Breathtaking Views: Go on exclusive luxury tour's to see the most gorgeous national parks with wildlife you'll only find here!
